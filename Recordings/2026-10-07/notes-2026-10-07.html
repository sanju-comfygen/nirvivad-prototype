Walkthrough notes

Presentation guide for the current prototype recording.

Flow covered

  1. Administration: overview, customer accounts, multiple roles and professional approval.
  2. Property Owner: dashboard, progress, information requests, KYC and subscription milestones.
  3. Internal property review: full record, FRD fields and documents, assignments and activity trail.
  4. Professional reviews: multiple Lawyers, CA, Broker, NV Channel Partner, Arbitrator and Builder.
  5. Selective sharing: specific property fields, report sections, team notes and optional report attachments.
  6. Follow-up rounds: a new Lawyer assignment preserves the earlier report and assignment history.
  7. Buyer enquiry: Buyer status, internal review, Broker and Partner support, follow-up round and audit trail.

Client decisions to confirm

  • Final role credentials, required documents and report fields.
  • Role-by-role permissions for shared report sections and attachments.
  • Follow-up review policy, due dates and closing rules.
  • Production authentication, durable audit, document storage, KYC and notification integrations.

The recording uses isolated sample accounts and sample property and enquiry records.
