# Nirvivad prototype walkthrough — 7 October 2026

This client-review edition uses live screenshots from an isolated browser profile with sample records. It updates the 6 October walkthrough while keeping the earlier edition for reference.

## Slide notes

01. **Administration overview** — The administration side holds complete records, team queues and role approvals.
02. **Customer accounts and roles** — One account can hold several roles. Super Admin reviews professional profiles and activates NV Channel Partners.
03. **Property Owner dashboard** — The Owner sees their own property, progress and only customer-facing actions.
04. **Property Owner progress** — Requested information, KYC and subscription milestones remain visible without internal findings.
05. **Property review: full record** — Administration can inspect the submitted property, Owner contact, KYC and property documents.
06. **Property review: requests** — Employee review selects missing FRD fields and documents, with response history and status controls.
07. **Multiple legal reviewers** — One property can have two Lawyers with independent scoped assignments and reports.
08. **Report-section sharing** — The team chooses individual report findings, team notes and an optional attachment for each recipient.
09. **Lawyer dashboard: two rounds** — The same Lawyer receives a new round after submitting the first report; the earlier round stays read-only.
10. **Lawyer follow-up review** — Round 2 shows new selected information and only the Broker report content explicitly shared.
11. **Lawyer previous-round history** — Earlier assignment details and the original submitted report remain available for context.
12. **Independent second Lawyer** — Another Lawyer can review the same property without receiving the first Lawyer’s private assignment.
13. **CA: selected findings only** — The CA sees selected Lawyer and Partner report sections, with only the attachment explicitly allowed.
14. **Broker property support** — The Broker sees only selected property details and submits market or site observations.
15. **NV Channel Partner support** — The admin-activated Partner sees assigned work and submits controlled transaction input.
16. **Arbitrator matter** — The Arbitrator reviews permitted dispute details and submits observations or an order.
17. **Builder / Developer response** — The Builder responds only on a related project or property assignment.
18. **Property activity trail** — Every assignment, report, information request, document and status change remains in Administration history.
19. **Buyer / Investor opportunities** — Buyers see approved, anonymised property summaries and submit interest through Nirvivad.
20. **Buyer enquiry status** — The Buyer sees their own enquiry and approved updates, without Owner or professional records.
21. **Admin buyer enquiry** — The team assigns, reviews and responds to Buyer interest in a separate enquiry workspace.
22. **Market support on an enquiry** — Broker and NV Partner assignments share only selected enquiry fields; contacts remain hidden.
23. **Broker enquiry: follow-up round** — A new Broker assignment after a submitted update creates Round 2, preserving the earlier response.
24. **Enquiry activity trail** — The Buyer and team actions, market assignments, updates and follow-up rounds are audited.

25. **Contact Us: linked to mobile** — Contact Us requires mobile, allows optional email, and lets a verified customer track the enquiry status set by Administration.
