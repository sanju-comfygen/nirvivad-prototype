How Nirvivad Works

A transparent path from a disputed property to a verified, resolved outcome.

1. Submit your property

Property owners begin with a short intake — your relationship to the property, its location, and the nature of the dispute. No lengthy forms and no payment at this stage.

2. Our team reviews your case

A case handler on our internal team reviews your submission and requests any additional details, documents, or identity verification (KYC) needed to proceed.

3. Independent expert verification

Depending on the nature of your dispute, we route your case to the right combination of professionals:

  • Lawyers review title deeds, legal rights, and court or arbitration records
  • Chartered Accountants assess valuation, taxation, and financial due diligence
  • Arbitrators review ongoing arbitration proceedings and tribunal orders where relevant
  • Brokers provide market and location verification

4. Verification decision

Once all required reviews are complete, your case is either approved and published anonymised on our Investor Marketplace, marked resolved without a public listing, or returned with a clear reason if it doesn't qualify.

5. Investor engagement

If your property goes live, verified investors can view a risk-scored summary and submit expressions of interest — all coordinated through our team. Your identity and contact details are never shared directly.

6. Resolution

Once a transaction is agreed or the dispute is otherwise resolved, your case is closed and archived, with a full audit trail retained internally.

About Nirvivad

Making disputed property transparent, verifiable, and investable.

Disputed property is one of the most difficult asset classes to transact in — sellers struggle to prove clean title, and buyers have no reliable way to assess risk. Nirvivad exists to close that gap.

We combine structured legal, financial and arbitration review with a transparent scoring model, so property owners get a fair resolution path and investors get verified opportunities they can actually trust.
